Workflow Explanation

Workflow Overview



The Smart JSA Automation workflow is organized into six sequential phases, preceded by the initial WhatsApp input.



The workflow transforms a JSA request into a reviewed, approved, and delivered JSA document.



The overall workflow is:



WhatsApp Input

&#x20;     ↓

Phase 1 — Job Information Collection

&#x20;     ↓

Phase 2 — JSA Draft \& AI-Assisted Hazard Analysis

&#x20;     ↓

Phase 3 — Safety Officer Review

&#x20;     ↓

Phase 4 — HSSE Manager Review

&#x20;     ↓

Phase 5 — Approval \& Registry Update

&#x20;     ↓

Phase 6 — Final JSA PDF Delivery

WhatsApp Input



The workflow begins when a Supervisor sends a message to the Smart JSA system through WhatsApp.



The incoming message acts as the entry point for the workflow.



At this stage, the system receives the initial request and begins processing the conversation.



The initial input is used to determine the next action required from the Supervisor.



Supervisor

&#x20;   ↓

WhatsApp Message

&#x20;   ↓

Smart JSA Workflow



Phase 1 — Job Information Collection



The first phase focuses on collecting the information required to prepare the JSA.



The system communicates with the Supervisor through WhatsApp and requests the required information about the planned job.



The Supervisor then provides the requested information.



The collected information becomes the input for the JSA drafting and analysis stage.



Main Activities

* Request job information.
* Receive the Supervisor's response.
* Process the submitted information.
* Prepare the data for the next phase.



Smart JSA System

&#x20;       ↓

Requests Job Information

&#x20;       ↓

Supervisor

&#x20;       ↓

Provides Job Information

&#x20;       ↓

Workflow Data



Phase 2 — JSA Draft and AI-Assisted Hazard Analysis



The second phase processes the submitted job information.



The system uses the collected information to prepare a JSA draft and perform AI-assisted hazard analysis.



The AI component supports the initial analysis of potential hazards and safety considerations associated with the submitted job information.



Main Activities

* Process job information.
* Generate a JSA draft.
* Perform AI-assisted hazard analysis.
* Structure the analysis output.
* Prepare the JSA for Safety Officer review.



Job Information

&#x20;       ↓

JSA Draft Generation

&#x20;       ↓

AI-Assisted Hazard Analysis

&#x20;       ↓

Structured JSA Analysis

&#x20;       ↓

JSA Draft



The result of this phase is a draft JSA that requires human review.



Phase 3 — Safety Officer Review



The third phase introduces technical K3 review.



The generated JSA draft and AI-assisted analysis are sent to the Safety Officer.



The Safety Officer reviews the content and evaluates whether the analysis is appropriate from a K3 perspective.



Main Activities

* Send the JSA draft for review.
* Present the AI-assisted analysis.
* Review identified hazards.
* Review proposed controls.
* Validate the JSA content.
* Approve or provide feedback according to the defined workflow.



JSA Draft

&#x20;   ↓

Safety Officer

&#x20;   ↓

K3 Review

&#x20;   ↓

Reviewed JSA



The Safety Officer acts as a critical human review point within the workflow.



Phase 4 — HSSE Manager Review



After the Safety Officer completes the review, the JSA proceeds to the HSSE Manager.



The system sends the reviewed JSA draft to the HSSE Manager for management-level review and approval.



Main Activities

* Route the reviewed JSA.
* Notify the HSSE Manager.
* Present the JSA for review.
* Receive the approval decision.



Safety Officer Review

&#x20;       ↓

Reviewed JSA

&#x20;       ↓

HSSE Manager

&#x20;       ↓

Management Review



This phase ensures that the JSA proceeds through the defined management approval process before final delivery.



Phase 5 — Approval and Registry Update



After the required approval process is completed, the workflow updates the relevant records.



The system updates the approval information and JSA Registry to reflect the current status of the JSA.



Main Activities

* Update approval status.
* Record approval information.
* Update JSA Registry information.
* Maintain the current JSA status.



Approved JSA

&#x20;     ↓

Approval Record Update

&#x20;     ↓

JSA Registry Update



This phase ensures that the JSA status is reflected in the relevant structured records.



Phase 6 — Final JSA PDF Delivery



The final phase focuses on delivering the approved JSA document.



After the HSSE Manager's approval, the system prepares the final JSA document as a PDF and sends it through WhatsApp.



Main Activities

* Retrieve the approved JSA information.
* Prepare the final document.
* Generate or obtain the final PDF.
* Send the approved JSA through WhatsApp.



HSSE Manager Approval

&#x20;       ↓

Final JSA Document

&#x20;       ↓

PDF

&#x20;       ↓

WhatsApp Delivery



The workflow is completed when the approved JSA PDF is delivered to the intended recipient.



Phase-to-Phase Transition



The workflow progresses through defined transitions.



WhatsApp Input → Phase 1



The initial JSA request is received and the system begins collecting the required job information.



Phase 1 → Phase 2



The required job information has been collected and is ready for JSA drafting and AI-assisted analysis.



Phase 2 → Phase 3



The JSA draft and AI-assisted analysis have been prepared and are ready for Safety Officer review.



Phase 3 → Phase 4



The Safety Officer has completed the review and the JSA proceeds to the HSSE Manager.



Phase 4 → Phase 5



The HSSE Manager has completed the required approval and the system updates the relevant records.



Phase 5 → Phase 6



The approval and JSA Registry records have been updated and the approved JSA proceeds to final document delivery.



Phase 6 → Complete



The final approved JSA PDF has been delivered through WhatsApp.



Workflow Control Logic



The workflow is designed to ensure that each major stage is completed before the process proceeds to the next stage.



The general control logic is:



Input

&#x20; ↓

Information Collection

&#x20; ↓

Analysis

&#x20; ↓

Safety Review

&#x20; ↓

Management Approval

&#x20; ↓

Record Update

&#x20; ↓

Final Delivery



This sequential structure helps maintain process order and ensures that the JSA passes through the required review stages.



Human Review Points



The workflow contains two major human review points.



Safety Officer



Reviews the JSA draft and AI-assisted analysis from a K3 perspective.



HSSE Manager



Reviews and approves the JSA at the management level.



These review points ensure that automated analysis and document generation remain subject to human oversight.



Workflow Outcome



The workflow produces a final approved JSA document that is:



* Reviewed by the Safety Officer.
* Approved by the HSSE Manager.
* Recorded in the relevant JSA Registry.
* Delivered as a PDF through WhatsApp.



The workflow therefore connects request initiation, information collection, AI-assisted analysis, human review, approval, record management, and final document delivery into one structured process.

Business Process

Process Overview



The Smart JSA Automation process transforms a JSA request into a reviewed, approved, and digitally delivered Job Safety Analysis document.



The process begins when a Supervisor submits a request through WhatsApp and ends when the final approved JSA is delivered as a PDF.

Process Actors

Supervisor



The Supervisor initiates the JSA request and provides the required information about the job or activity.



The Supervisor is responsible for providing accurate information about the planned work so that the JSA can be prepared based on the actual job conditions.



Smart JSA Automation System



The system manages the automated workflow, including:



* Receiving and processing requests.
* Collecting job information.
* Generating JSA drafts.
* Supporting AI-assisted hazard analysis.
* Routing the JSA for review and approval.
* Updating records.
* Delivering the final document.



Safety Officer



The Safety Officer reviews the generated JSA draft and AI-assisted analysis from a K3 perspective.



The Safety Officer acts as a human review point to validate the safety analysis before the JSA proceeds to the next approval stage.



HSSE Manager



The HSSE Manager reviews the JSA after the Safety Officer's review and provides the required management-level approval.



Process Stages

WhatsApp Input



The process begins when the Supervisor contacts the Smart JSA system through WhatsApp to initiate a JSA request.



The request acts as the entry point into the automated workflow.

Phase 1 — Job Information Collection



The system communicates with the Supervisor to collect the information required to prepare the JSA.



The collected information may include details related to the planned job, work location, work activity, and other information required by the JSA process.

&#x20;       ↓

Supervisor

The collected information becomes the basis for the subsequent JSA drafting and analysis process.



Phase 2 — JSA Draft and AI-Assisted Hazard Analysis



Based on the submitted job information, the system generates a JSA draft and performs AI-assisted hazard analysis.



The AI is used to support the initial identification and analysis of potential hazards associated with the submitted work information.

&#x20;       ↓

&#x20;       ↓

The output of this stage is not considered the final approved JSA.



It is prepared for review by the Safety Officer.



Phase 3 — Safety Officer Review



The JSA draft and AI-assisted analysis are sent to the Safety Officer through the workflow communication channel.



The Safety Officer reviews the draft and evaluates the AI-assisted analysis from a K3 perspective.

This stage maintains human involvement in the safety decision-making process.



Phase 4 — HSSE Manager Review and Approval



After the Safety Officer completes the review, the JSA is routed to the HSSE Manager.



The HSSE Manager reviews the JSA and provides the required approval for the next stage of the process.

Phase 5 — Approval and Registry Update



After the approval process, the system updates the relevant records.



The update may include:



* JSA approval status.
* Approval information.
* JSA registry information.
* Relevant workflow records.

Phase 6 — Final JSA Delivery



After the HSSE Manager's approval, the system prepares the final JSA document as a PDF.



The final approved document is then delivered through WhatsApp.

End-to-End Process



The complete process can be summarized as follows:



1. Supervisor initiates a JSA request

The Supervisor contacts the Smart JSA system through WhatsApp.

2\. WhatsApp Input

The system receives the initial JSA request.

3\. Phase 1 — Job Information Collection

The system requests the required job information from the Supervisor.

The Supervisor provides the requested information.

4\. Phase 2 — JSA Draft and AI-Assisted Hazard Analysis

The system generates a JSA draft.

AI performs an initial hazard analysis based on the submitted job information.

5\. Phase 3 — Safety Officer Review

The JSA draft and AI-assisted analysis are sent to the Safety Officer.

The Safety Officer reviews the content from a K3 perspective.

6\. Phase 4 — HSSE Manager Review and Approval

The reviewed JSA is sent to the HSSE Manager.

The HSSE Manager reviews and provides the required approval.

7\. Phase 5 — Approval and Registry Update

The system updates the approval records.

The system updates the JSA Registry.

8\. Phase 6 — Final JSA Delivery

The approved JSA is prepared as a final PDF.

The final document is delivered through WhatsApp.



AI-Assisted Analysis

&#x20;       ↓

Safety Officer Review

&#x20;       ↓

HSSE Manager Approval

&#x20;       ↓

Final JSA Delivery



AI supports the analysis process, while the Safety Officer and HSSE Manager retain responsibility for human review and approval according to the applicable organizational process.



Process Outcome



The final outcome of the process is an approved JSA document that has:



* Passed the defined review and approval process.
* Been recorded in the relevant JSA registry.
* Been converted into a final PDF document.
* Been delivered to the intended recipient through WhatsApp.



The process creates a structured workflow from JSA request to final approved document delivery.
